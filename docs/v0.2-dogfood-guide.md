## 4. Product Owner acceptance flow

1. Open **My Job Missions** and confirm the seeded Mission or choose **Create
   Mission**.
2. Paste one synthetic or real JD. Only JD raw text is required. Confirm the
   detected company and role and read **What Matters for This Role**.
3. On Resume, generate Experience Selection, review KEEP_AND_HIGHLIGHT / KEEP /
   DEEMPHASIZE / OMIT, then generate Resume Strategy and Target Resume v1.
4. Accept, edit, or reject bullets. Run **Stress-test my resume** and read each
   claim risk and next step.
5. On Proof, generate Claim Analysis. For a weak claim, try **Recover existing
   evidence** first. If it was not done, start a Proof Sprint, complete its
   artifact through the existing proof flow, and re-evaluate the claim.
6. Generate Target Resume v2 after evidence is stronger. The master Profile is
   not overwritten.
7. Build the Company-specific Interview Pack. Continue to the adaptive mock
   interview; answer at least three rounds and finish when ready (the backend
   accepts up to eight rounds). Read the debrief gap type and explanation.
8. Record a simulated Outcome. Leave the curated-intelligence checkbox off
   unless the feedback is explicitly approved for future retrieval.
9. Return to Overview and verify readiness reasons, proof actions, and status.

## 5. Company retrieval and multiple Missions

Company packs are synthetic demo data for Baidu, Xiaohongshu, and ByteDance.
Retrieval ranks same company + role family, same company related role, same
role family at another company, and generic AI PM packs. Every result keeps its
pack ID, source count, recency, confidence, and refs.

Create a second Mission for the same Profile. Its What Matters, Resume, Red
Team, Interview Pack, and proof snapshot must use its own target job. Global
Profile facts are shared. Changing a bullet in Mission A must not change Mission
B.

## 6. Add a skill without code changes

Add a Markdown file under `knowledge/resume_skills/` or
`knowledge/interview_skills/`:

```markdown
---
id: baidu_metrics_v2
type: interview
version: 1
source_name: synthetic-demo
source_urls: [https://example.test/source]
latest_source_date: 2026-09
confidence: medium
company: Baidu
role_family: AI_PRODUCT
competencies: [PRODUCT_METRICS]
source_count: 1
---
Observed signal, question patterns, follow-up patterns, and evidence expected.
```

`type: resume` and `type: interview` are normalized to the internal
`resume_skill` / `interview_skill` kinds. `kind` and `source_refs` are also
accepted. A malformed file is reported as a diagnostic while valid packs keep
loading. Reload or sync after editing; the SHA-256 fingerprint changes with
the file.
