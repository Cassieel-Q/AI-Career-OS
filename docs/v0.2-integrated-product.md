# AI Career OS v0.2 Integrated Dogfood

## Product model

`JobMission` is the user-visible preparation unit for one target JD. It owns the
company, role detection, JD projection, What Matters, interview intelligence,
experience choices, Target Resume versions, Red Team reports, Interview Packs,
and real interview outcomes. `TargetJob` remains the durable JD record. A
Profile, its master facts, projects, and artifacts remain global and are shared
by every Mission.

## Three intelligence layers

- **Career Evidence Intelligence** answers what the candidate actually did. It
  reads Profile facts, Projects, artifacts, user-confirmed evidence, proof
  outputs, and outcome notes.
- **Resume Intelligence** decides how verified experience should be expressed
  for this JD. Built-in Resume Skills are guidance, never evidence.
- **Interview Intelligence** describes observed company and role signals. It is
  retrieved from structured Knowledge Packs and is shown as “Observed in N
  curated reports”; it never predicts a question as certain.

Provider calls use JSON mode followed by explicit JSON parsing and strict
Pydantic validation. A provider response cannot create a material claim unless
the claim is tied to Profile or artifact evidence. Provider work happens before
the persistence transaction is committed.

## Job Mission flow

`Paste JD → detect company/role → What Matters → company interview retrieval →
experience selection → resume strategy → Target Resume v1 → Accept/Edit/Reject
bullets → Red Team → Recover Existing Evidence or Proof Sprint → artifact →
re-evaluate → Target Resume v2 → Interview Pack → adaptive mock interview (3–8
rounds) → debrief → readiness → real outcome.`

Existing proof and workflow routes remain available for legacy access. The v0.2
home page is **My Job Missions**, with five Mission tabs: Overview, Resume,
Proof, Interview, and Outcome. Mission proof snapshots are scoped to the
Mission target job so adding a second JD cannot change the first Mission's
interview or proof state.

## Readiness language

The Overview reports `READY_TO_APPLY`, `STRENGTHEN_FIRST`, or
`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` with reasons, blocks, and what would change the advice.
It does not produce an ATS-style score and does not decide whether the user
should apply.

## Full Intelligence layers (v0.2)

| Layer | Role | Where |
|-------|------|-------|
| Career Evidence Intelligence | What the candidate really did | Profile facts + Proof + Evidence OS gates (`011_evidence_os`) |
| Resume Intelligence | How to select/write for a JD | `knowledge/resume_skills` (+ CURATED_RESEARCH boundary / AMR packs) |
| Interview Intelligence | How companies validate | `knowledge/interview_skills/curated/*` + demo/SYNTHETIC packs |
| Job Mission | Orchestrates one JD end-to-end | Primary UX — Missions nav |

Evidence OS UI stays **Advanced/Debug** at `/evidence-os` — never a required mission step.

### Provenance

- `CURATED` — distilled from real Nowcoder / research corpus
- `CURATED_RESEARCH` — mechanism principles from aicareer-profiles (no third-party skill prose)
- `SYNTHETIC` / `SYNTHETIC_DEMO` — demo packs only; must stay labeled

### Company relevance tiers

Retriever should prefer `SAME_COMPANY` > `ROLE_FAMILY` > `GENERIC`. UI should show these labels when Interview Pack cards exist.
